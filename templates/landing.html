Is there a free trial? +
Yes — every plan starts with a 30-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full Growth plan access during the trial.
Can I import my existing leads? +
Yes. CLRT supports CSV import for leads. You can map your existing columns during the import process.
How many users can I add? +
Starter: 2 users, Growth: 5 users, Enterprise: unlimited. Each user gets their own login and individual activity tracking.
Does it work for teams in Nepal and India? +
Absolutely. CLRT supports Rs. (Indian Rupee), NPR (Nepalese Rupee), USD, GBP, and more — all configurable from your Settings page.
Can I accept payments online? +
Yes — Growth and Enterprise plans include Razorpay integration. Clients can pay invoices via UPI, cards, or netbanking directly from the invoice page.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel your subscription at any time from the Subscription page inside the app.
